The Challenge

📈

Rising Incidence

CRC incidence is increasing among young adults under 50, with China accounting for ~28.8% of new cases worldwide and ~30.6% of global CRC-related deaths.

🎯

Limited Response

~85% of CRC patients have MSS/pMMR tumors that respond poorly to current immune checkpoint inhibitors due to immunologically "cold" tumor microenvironments.

🧬

Complex Biology

Tumor microenvironment heterogeneity, immunosuppressive networks (Tregs, MDSCs, CAFs), and acquired resistance mechanisms limit treatment efficacy.

Two Immunophenotypes

~15%

MSI-H / dMMR
  • High tumor mutational burden (TMB)
  • Rich in neoantigens
  • Strong T-cell infiltration (TILs)
  • Excellent ICI response
  • FDA-approved first-line immunotherapy
~85%

❄️ "Cold" Tumors

MSS / pMMR
  • Low tumor mutational burden
  • Limited neoantigen production
  • Sparse immune infiltration
  • Immunosuppressive TME
  • Poor ICI monotherapy response

Current Therapeutic Strategies

PD-1/PD-L1 Inhibitors

Pembrolizumab (KEYNOTE-177) established as first-line treatment for MSI-H/dMMR mCRC with significant PFS improvement and 11.1% CR rate.

Dual Checkpoint Blockade

Nivolumab + Ipilimumab achieved 69% ORR and 13% CR rate in first-line MSI-H/dMMR patients (CheckMate 142).

Neoadjuvant Immunotherapy

NICHE study: 100% pathological response, 60% pCR in early-stage dMMR colon cancer. Dostarlimab: 100% cCR in locally advanced dMMR rectal cancer.

Anti-angiogenic Combinations

REGONIVO trial: Regorafenib + Nivolumab achieved 33.3% ORR in MSS/pMMR CRC through TME remodeling and vascular normalization.

Radiotherapy + Immunotherapy

VOLTAGE study: CRT + Nivolumab achieved 30% pCR in MSS locally advanced rectal cancer by inducing immunogenic cell death.

Targeted + Immune Combinations

BRAF/MEK inhibitors + PD-1 blockade (DTP regimen): 25% cORR in MSS BRAF-V600E CRC through MAPK pathway modulation.

Emerging Frontiers

🧪 Adoptive Cell Therapy

CAR-T cells targeting CEA, EGFR, HER2, GUCY2C; TCR-T therapy based on neoantigen recognition; NK cell therapies for personalized immune intervention.

Global Accessibility Challenge

⚠️ The Equity Gap

Despite breakthrough efficacy, immunotherapies face significant accessibility barriers worldwide. High treatment costs place them beyond reach for most patients in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). Even in high-income countries, restrictive reimbursement policies often delay or deny access. Structural challenges including lack of biosimilars, cold-chain logistics, and shortages of specialized personnel further exacerbate global disparities.

Conclusion

Immunotherapy has ushered in a transformative era for MSI-H/dMMR CRC patients (~15%), establishing new standards of care with unprecedented response rates and durable remissions. However, the vast majority (~85%) with MSS/pMMR tumors remain largely refractory due to immunologically "cold" microenvironments. The path forward demands a dual commitment: deepening mechanistic understanding of immune resistance to develop biomarker-informed combinatorial regimens, while ensuring that scientific advances are accessible to all patients, regardless of geography or socioeconomic status.
